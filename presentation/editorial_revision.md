# Editorial Revision — V15

## Presentation strategy

The deck now distinguishes four levels that had previously been compressed together:

1. **Historical conditions** — the changing conditions of symbols, trust, and subjectivity before they become formal research questions.
2. **Life choice** — the career turn as an aesthetic judgment about where to devote one's life, while normative judgment remains a distinct scholarly object.
3. **Scholarly method** — problems remain central; disciplinary methods are composed according to the object. GFT linguistics is presented only as one example.
4. **Economic formalization** — GRE is shown through value generation, circulation, return, and reproduction rather than through dense manuscript screenshots.

The former “職務に関連する経験” slide was removed because it repeated information already evident from the CV and weakened the intellectual arc.
