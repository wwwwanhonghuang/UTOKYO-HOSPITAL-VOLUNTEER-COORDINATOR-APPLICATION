## Main Responsibilities

### 1. Volunteer Operations and Coordination
- Plan, publicize, and conduct volunteer recruitment, selection, and training
- Monitor and manage volunteer activities
- Respond to volunteer consultations
- Coordinate with relevant hospital departments
- Maintain activity records
- Prepare reports
- Plan and operate recognition / commendation activities

### 2. Support for Volunteer Members
- Administrative support related to health management, including:
  - health checks
  - antibody testing
  - vaccination
  - guidance
  - scheduling
  - record organization
- Improve and maintain the volunteer activity environment

### 3. Support for Hospital Services
- Assist visitors with reception and hospital navigation
- Support wheelchair users
- Assist with operation of the hospital library, **にこにこ文庫プラス**

### 4. Hospital Events
- Support, plan, and assist in operating hospital events

### 5. Patient Services
- Support and improve patient services generally

### 6. General Administrative Work
- Perform other administrative work within the Medical Operations Division
- Reassignment or concurrent duties may be ordered
